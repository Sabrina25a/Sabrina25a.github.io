Smart Building Management System

A Raspberry Pi Pico W-based smart building management system designed to improve monitoring, control, and accessibility within a university building.

01

Overview

The Smart Building Management System was developed as part of ENGR 120 at the University of Victoria. The project explored how an integrated sensing and control system could improve the usability and accessibility of building environments. As part of the project, we developed and documented SmartZone, an MVP proof of concept for a smart building management system.

SmartZone uses a Raspberry Pi Pico W, environmental sensors, and a user interface to monitor building conditions and provide real-time feedback and control. The prototype demonstrates how hardware and software components can be integrated to collect sensor data, process information, and support building management functions.

My Contribution

BuildHers

I collaborated with the BuildHers team to document the SmartZone design in the project's technical report. The report compares SmartZone against two alternative designs and evaluates the solutions based on factors including accessibility, usability, reliability, and system integration.

Integrated Systems Design Team

I also worked with the Integrated Systems Design Team to build and integrate the physical prototype for SmartZone. This included connecting the system components and developing the prototype for the final demonstration.

03

SmartZone

SmartZone is a microcontroller-based building management system developed using a Raspberry Pi Pico W. Our team designed and built a minimum viable product (MVP) proof of concept that demonstrated the core sensing, monitoring, and control functions of the proposed system.

The prototype integrates multiple sensors with a user interface to monitor environmental conditions and provide real-time feedback. The project focused on system reliability, usability, accessibility, and integration across hardware and software components.
